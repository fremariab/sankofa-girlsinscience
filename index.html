WHAT WE DO

01.

SGiS Mentoring & Career Counseling

Connecting students with professional women in STEM to provide academic insights, life skills, and the confidence to set "big, hairy, audacious" goals.

02.

Science Clinics & Workshops

Intensive sessions designed to prepare graduating students for final exams and hands-on science workshops that reach girls as young as 11.

03.

Resource Centers

Dedication of a fully equipped Science Library and Reading Room at Breman Asikuma Senior High School to provide up-to-date textbooks and learning materials.

Soon launching: A tech lab for girls in the heart of Tema.
